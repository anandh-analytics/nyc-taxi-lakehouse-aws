## Findings (January 2023 sample)
- Revenue concentration is driven by a small set of premium pickup zones with high average fares.
- Airport-dependent zones (identified as 132, 138, 70) show near-total dependency on airport traffic.
- Scenario simulation (50% airport drop) shows CRITICAL_RISK zones suffer disproportionate revenue loss.
- SAFE zones are diversified: low airport dependency and stable revenue base.
